Career Options After 12th Standard

Discover undergraduate courses and career paths after completing your 12th grade

Career Paths Based on Your Stream

For Science Students

Engineering

Pursue B.Tech/B.E in various disciplines such as:

  • Computer Science Engineering
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Electrical Engineering
  • Civil Engineering
  • Information Technology

Entrance Exams: JEE Main, JEE Advanced, State-level Engineering Entrance Tests

Medical & Healthcare

Options include:

  • MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine and Surgery)
  • BDS (Bachelor of Dental Surgery)
  • BAMS (Ayurveda)
  • B.Pharm (Pharmacy)
  • BSc Nursing

Entrance Exams: NEET, AIIMS, JIPMER

Pure Sciences

Bachelor's degrees in:

  • BSc Physics
  • BSc Chemistry
  • BSc Mathematics
  • BSc Biology
  • BSc Computer Science

Admissions: Merit-based or through university entrance tests

Other Options
  • Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch)
  • Bachelor of Design (B.Des)
  • BSc Agriculture
  • Merchant Navy
  • Defense Services (NDA)
  • Pilot Training

Entrance Exams: NATA (for B.Arch), UCEED (for B.Des), NDA exam, etc.

For Commerce Students

Accounting & Finance

Career paths include:

  • B.Com (Bachelor of Commerce)
  • BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)
  • Chartered Accountancy (CA)
  • Company Secretary (CS)
  • CMA (Cost Management Accountant)

Entrance Exams: University entrance tests, CA Foundation, CS Foundation

Management & Business

Options include:

  • BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)
  • BBM (Bachelor of Business Management)
  • BMS (Bachelor of Management Studies)
  • B.Com in Banking & Insurance
  • Bachelor of Foreign Trade

Entrance Exams: IPMAT, DU JAT, various university entrance tests

Economics & Statistics

Pursue degrees in:

  • BA Economics
  • BSc Economics
  • BSc Statistics
  • Bachelor of Economic Studies
  • Integrated Economics programs

Admissions: Merit-based or through entrance tests

Law & Related Fields
  • BA LLB (5-year integrated program)
  • BBA LLB (5-year integrated program)
  • B.Com LLB (5-year integrated program)
  • Company Secretary

Entrance Exams: CLAT, AILET, LSAT, various university law entrance tests

For Arts/Humanities Students

Liberal Arts & Social Sciences

Bachelor's degrees in:

  • BA English/Literature
  • BA History
  • BA Political Science
  • BA Sociology
  • BA Psychology
  • BA Philosophy

Admissions: Merit-based or through university entrance tests

Media & Communication

Options include:

  • BA Journalism & Mass Communication
  • Bachelor of Journalism
  • BA in Media Studies
  • BA in Film Studies
  • Bachelor of Mass Media (BMM)

Entrance Exams: University-specific entrance tests

Design & Creative Arts

Pursue degrees in:

  • Bachelor of Design (B.Des)
  • Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA)
  • Bachelor of Performing Arts
  • Fashion Design
  • Interior Design

Entrance Exams: NID, NIFT, UCEED, and other design entrance tests

Hotel Management & Tourism
  • Bachelor of Hotel Management (BHM)
  • Bachelor of Tourism Management
  • BA in Hospitality Management
  • Bachelor of Tourism Studies

Entrance Exams: NCHMCT JEE, university-specific entrance tests
