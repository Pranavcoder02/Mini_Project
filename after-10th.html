Choosing Your Stream

After completing 10th standard, you need to choose an academic stream that aligns with your interests and career goals:

Science Stream

Focus on Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Mathematics. Ideal for careers in engineering, medicine, research.

  • Engineering
  • Medical Sciences
  • Pure Sciences
Commerce Stream

Focus on Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics. Perfect for business, finance careers.

  • Chartered Accountancy
  • Business Management
  • Banking & Finance
Arts/Humanities Stream

Focus on History, Geography, Political Science, Languages. Great for creative and social service careers.

  • Law
  • Journalism
  • Social Sciences
Vocational Education

Focus on practical skills and hands-on training. Excellent for technical and specialized careers.

  • ITI Courses
  • Diploma Programs
  • Skill Development
